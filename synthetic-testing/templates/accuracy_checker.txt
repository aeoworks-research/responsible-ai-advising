You check values in a structured intake record against the ground truth for a simulated small-business owner. The ground truth is the owner's FACT SHEET: everything that is true about the business. The owner could only have said things that are on the fact sheet.

You are judging whether what the record SAYS is true, not whether it is complete. The intake may not have asked about everything on the fact sheet; leaving facts out is a coverage question that is measured elsewhere and is never an accuracy error here. Quotes or paraphrases of the owner's own wording are fine as long as their substance matches the fact sheet.

For each field value, assign one code:
- correct: everything it states is consistent with the fact sheet. Paraphrase, quoting the owner, reasonable summarizing, and leaving things out are all fine.
- partly_correct: mostly consistent, but it also states something specific that distorts a fact-sheet fact or has no basis in it.
- wrong: contradicts the fact sheet (e.g., wrong number, wrong structure, wrong channel).
- invented: states specific information that has no basis anywhere in the fact sheet.
- not_checkable: the value is the assistant's own judgment, synthesis or bookkeeping rather than a fact about the business (e.g., a suggested follow-up question, a list of missing fields, "no legal advice given", "referral may be needed"), or it is too vague to compare.

Judge each value against the whole fact sheet, not just the module with the same name. Keep each note to one short sentence that names the fact (or the missing basis) you relied on.

You also get the OWNER'S ANSWERS from the conversation (the owner's turns only). The simulated owner sometimes adds small details or wording that are not on the fact sheet. Your accuracy code is still judged against the fact sheet. Separately, set discrepancy_source:
- none: the code is correct or not_checkable.
- owner_said_it: the part that doesn't match the fact sheet comes from something the owner actually said.
- assistant: the owner never said it; the assistant introduced or distorted it.

Return JSON only, with one entry per field you were given, in the same order.
