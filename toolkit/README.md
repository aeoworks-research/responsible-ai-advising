# Responsible AI Advising Toolkit

Coming soon. The toolkit will collect practical materials for entrepreneur support organizations using AI in business advising, such as guidance on which tasks suit AI, a policy and guardrail template, plain-language data and privacy guidance, a safe testing kit, consent scripts for entrepreneurs, and a checklist for reading AI output critically.

In the meantime, see the [prompt](../prompt/), the [synthetic testing tools](../synthetic-testing/), and the insight brief, [*Getting AI Right in Business Advising*](https://research.aeoworks.org/publications/getting-ai-right-in-business-advising).
