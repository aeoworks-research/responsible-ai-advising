# Responsible AI Advising Toolkit

**Version 1.0 · October 2026**

A practical guide for business support organizations using AI to prepare for advising, while keeping people in charge of judgment, relationships and decisions. It is built from AEO's 2026 co-design and testing of an AI-supported business intake.

**Get the toolkit:** https://research.aeoworks.org/publications/responsible-ai-advising

That page always has the latest version. The toolkit is not stored in this repository, so check there for updates.

## What's inside

- **Getting started:** what we did and what we learned, and where to start depending on whether your organization is exploring, testing or already using AI.
- **Part 1, for organization leaders:** is AI right for this task; a policy and guardrail template; a data and privacy guide; testing safely before launch, including five pressure tests; choosing a deployment path; measuring what matters; and what to do when something goes wrong.
- **Part 2, for advisors:** talking with entrepreneurs about AI (consent scripts), reading an AI handoff critically, working with the intake prompt, and lessons from practitioners.
- **Part 3, keeping it responsible over time:** when to re-test, who owns the work, and how the toolkit applies AEO's Main Street AI Principles.
